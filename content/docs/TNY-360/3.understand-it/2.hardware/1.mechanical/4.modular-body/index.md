# Modular body design

The TNY-360 is divided into physical modules so that the robot can be assembled, disassembled, customized, and repaired without treating the entire body as one permanent shell.

---

## Separation points

The main separation points include:

- the head;
- the torso;
- the back;
- the battery area.

These areas provide access to different electronics and make it possible to modify one part without redesigning every other part.

## Fasteners and inserts

M3 inserts and screws hold the structural modules together, where a clip would not provide enough strength or repeatability.

This combines convenient access with a reliable mechanical connection for load-bearing parts.

## Customization and maintenance

This modular design makes it easier to replace or upgrade parts of the robot, and allows for more advanced customization.

For example, the head can be replaced with a different design (or removed entirely) without needing to redesign the torso or back parts.
