# Mechanical design

The mechanical design of the TNY-360 is built around four goals: keeping the robot rigid while it moves, protecting the actuators during unexpected impacts, making the body easy to assemble and customize, and providing useful physical references for calibration.

This section introduces the mechanical choices that shape the robot's behavior.
