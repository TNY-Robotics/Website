# Clip system

The TNY-360 uses clips to attach almost all aesthetic parts to the structural body.

Here's how the clip system works, why it is used, and what to consider when assembling or replacing clipped parts.

---

## Why use clips?

Clips reduce the number of permanent fasteners needed for parts that may need to be removed regularly.

This reduces the cost of the robot, while making it easier to replace and customize parts, and helps protect the robot during falls.

### As a quicker assembly method

Clips allow aesthetic parts to be attached without screws or other fasteners. This makes assembly and disassembly faster, and reduces the number of tools needed to work on the robot.

### As mechanical fuses

The clip system provides a controlled weak point during a fall. When an aesthetic part is pulled or struck, it may release before a more expensive structural component, motor, or PCB is damaged.

**This does not make every fall harmless**, but it is a design strategy that can reduce secondary damage and make the robot easier to restore after an impact.

---
