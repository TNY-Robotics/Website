# Modding the motors

One of the best features of the TNY-360 is its position feedback on every one of its MG996R servomotors.

This feature is made possible by the use of a small PCB containing an operational amplifier, which outputs the position of the motor shaft as an analog voltage.

Since you're building your own TNY-360, let's see how to mod your MG996R servomotors.
