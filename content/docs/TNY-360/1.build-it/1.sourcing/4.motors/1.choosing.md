# Choosing the right PCB

MG996R may seem identical on the outside, but their internal design can vary a lot depending on the manufacturer.

Depending on your type of MG996R, you will have to use a different version of the Servo Feedback Board. Here's how to identify which version of the PCB you need to use:

### Check the motor's PCB

Look at the PCB inside the MG996R. Depending on which type of PCB it is, you will have to use the V1 or V2 version of the Servo Feedback Board.

- If the PCB is at the top of the servomotor and is directly soldered to the motor, then you will need to use the V1 version.

- If the PCB is at the bottom of the servomotor and has wires going to the motor, you will need the V2 version.
