# The Buck Converter

We are done playing now... The Buck Converter is the last PCB you'll have to solder for your TNY-360, but also the most important one.

This board handles a lot of current, and is crucial for the proper functioning of your robot. Don't rush, and double check the orientation of the components, and how much solder paste you apply on the pads.
