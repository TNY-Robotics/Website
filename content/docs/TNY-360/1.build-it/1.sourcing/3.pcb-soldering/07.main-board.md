# The Main Board

We are entering the big finale of the PCB soldering! The Main Board is one of the most complex PCBs of the TNY-360.

Take your time, and make sure to double check the orientation of the components, connectors, and to apply the right amount of solder paste on the pads to avoid solder bridges (especially on the USB-C and Camera connectors) !
