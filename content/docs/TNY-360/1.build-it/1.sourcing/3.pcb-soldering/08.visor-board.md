# The Visor Board

Take your time with this one ... The Visor Board doesn't have a lot of components, but some of them are very small, and the solder pads are tiny.

Double check the orientation of the laser sensor (VL53L0x) and the microphone (MSM261) before soldering them, and how much solder paste you apply on the pads :)
