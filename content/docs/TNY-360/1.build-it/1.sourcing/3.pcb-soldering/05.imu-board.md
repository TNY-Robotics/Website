# The IMU Board

We are halfway through the PCBs! Now is the time to solder the IMU Board, which has one tricky component on it : the IMU itself.

The LSM6DS3 IMU is small, with very small solder pads, and they are underneath the component, so you won't be able to see them once the IMU is soldered.

Make sure to double check the orientation of the IMU, and to apply the right amount of solder paste on the pads, otherwise the IMU may not work properly, or not work at all.
