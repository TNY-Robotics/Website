# Soldering the PCBs


## PCBs overview

Here's what PCBs you'll need to solder for your TNY-360:

:section-button{href="./ext-board" title="The Extension Board" desc="The first and simplest PCB you'll need to solder."}

:section-button{href="./power-board" title="The Power Board" desc="The PCB responsible for distributing battery power to your TNY-360."}

:section-button{href="./motor-driver" title="The Motor Driver" desc="The PCB that controls the motors, and plugs into the backbone."}

:section-button{href="./analog-reader" title="The Analog Reader" desc="The PCB that reads position feedback, and plugs into the backbone."}

:section-button{href="./imu-board" title="The IMU Board" desc="The PCB that measures your TNY-360's orientation, and plugs into the backbone."}

:section-button{href="./main-board" title="The Main Board" desc="The central PCB that powers your TNY-360 brain with its ESP32-S3."}

:section-button{href="./visor-board" title="The Visor Board" desc="The PCB used for the TNY-360's front visor, which holds the screen."}

:section-button{href="./backbone" title="The Backbone" desc="The PCB that gathers and routes signals from all the components of your TNY-360."}

:section-button{href="./buck-conv" title="The Buck Converter" desc="The PCB that converts battery power to the required robot's voltages."}
