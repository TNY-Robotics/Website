# The Motor Driver

Two boards done, eight to go! The Motor Driver is the third PCB you'll need to solder for your TNY-360.

This board is a little more complex as it has a few more components to solder, and some of them have small solder pads.
But with patience and a steady hand, you'll be able to solder it without any problem !

::info-box{title="LED Orientation" type="tip"}
If you struggle to find the orientation of the LEDs, try matching the green arrow at the back of the LED with the one on the silkscreen of the PCB !
::
