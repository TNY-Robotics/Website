::info-box{title="Cable orientation" type="warning"}
When ordering the cables, **pay close attention to the orientation of the connectors**.

The two ends of a cable can be connected in two ways:
1. **Same** - the connectors on both ends have the same orientation (e.g. both with the latch facing up).
2. **Reversed** - the connectors on both ends have opposite orientations (e.g. one with the latch facing up and the other with the latch facing down).

For the TNY-360 build, you will need **ONLY *REVERSED*** cables, because this orientation keeps the pin order consistent across all connectors (GND is always first).<br>
**DO NOT BUY *SAME ORIENTATION* CABLES**, as this may cause short circuits and damage your components because you'll connect the pins in the wrong order (e.g. GND to VCC).
