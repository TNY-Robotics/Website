# The Torso

The torso requires a total of 24 heat inserts to be placed in the printed parts.

Yes, this is a lot, but it is the main structural part of the robot, and it needs to be strong and durable to withstand the forces and stresses during operation.

---

There are **20 Heat Inserts on the Bottom** part of the torso, and **4 Heat Inserts on the Top** part of the Torso.
