# Development & Modding

Welcome to the TNY-360 development and modding hub!

This section is dedicated to users who want to build around the TNY-360, whether by creating custom attachments, modifying the robot's design, or developing new software features.

The TNY-360 is designed to be a flexible platform. You can extend it physically through the universal mount, or interact with its hardware and systems through the available SDKs and API.
