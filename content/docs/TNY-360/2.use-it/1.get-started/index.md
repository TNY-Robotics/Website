# Getting started

Let's wake up your TNY-360 and get it ready for its first movements!

---

## 1 - Turn on your TNY-360

Make sure your battery is connected, then turn on your TNY-360.

After a few seconds, the robot's display will show a QR code. This code allows you to connect to the robot and access its web interface.

## 2 - Run the auto-diagnostic

Scan the QR code with your phone. You will be redirected to the **Diagnostic** page of the TNY-360 web interface.

Launch the auto-diagnostic from this page and wait for it to complete. The robot will check that its main components are working correctly.

::info-box{title="If the diagnostic fails" type="warning"}
Do not continue to the next step until the issue has been resolved. Check the diagnostic results and follow the appropriate troubleshooting instructions.
::

Once the auto-diagnostic is complete, the robot will reboot automatically.

## 3 - Connect to the web interface

When the robot has finished rebooting, scan the new QR code displayed on its screen to connect to it again.

This time, you will be redirected to the main web interface of your TNY-360.

## 4 - Run the auto-calibration

The web interface will prompt you to calibrate your robot. Accept the prompt and launch the auto-calibration.

Auto-calibration adjusts the motors and sensors so that your TNY-360 can move correctly and perform at its best.

The calibration can take up to **15 minutes**. Keep the robot still and do not turn it off while the process is running.

::info-box{title="If the calibration fails" type="warning"}
Do not use the robot until the calibration issue has been resolved. Check the calibration results and follow the appropriate troubleshooting instructions.
::

## You're ready!

Once the auto-calibration is complete, your TNY-360 is ready for its first movements!

Continue with the [First Steps](../teleoperation) guide to learn how to control your robot using a gamepad or a keyboard.
